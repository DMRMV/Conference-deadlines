Sorting. The default order is by next milestone, earliest first. Events whose only remaining date is the meeting itself sit below those, and events with no announced deadline sit at the bottom. Any column header re-sorts the table.

Regions. The region tag follows the physical host city, so hybrid meetings are filed under the region they take place in and can still be reached through the Europe, North America or APAC filters. Virtual-only meetings sit in their own Virtual group rather than being assigned a geography. Australia covers Australia and New Zealand; the rest of Asia-Pacific is APAC; Mexico, Central America, the Caribbean and South America are Latin America. Sorting the location column groups by region, then by date. Region and relevance filters combine.

Dates. Nothing here is estimated. Where a deadline has not been published, the cell says "Not yet announced" rather than guessing. Deadlines shift — confirm on the linked page before you plan around one. The small grey line under each status is the date that row was last checked against its source.
